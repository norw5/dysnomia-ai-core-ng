## 2. Role

A message-layer stream cipher between two registered users: `Encrypt(from,
to, key, data)` XORs the plaintext against a keystream generated by
ALTERNATING Rod/Cone React steps between the two parties' SHIOs; ciphertexts
expire in 10 days and are pruned. Party-gated both directions (`NotParty`
unless the caller's SHIO ownership checks pass). `Saat(bytes)` folds any
bytes into three uint64 "chromosomes" (the ≥18-byte input check).

**Security posture**: NOT confidentiality in
the cryptographic sense — the keystream is a public orbit over world-readable
state (SHA.View exposes Secret; MotzkinPrime ≈ 2^49.8). What it provides:
permissioning (only the two parties' SHIO owners), co-evolution commitment
(the keystream depends on the pair's actual shared call history —
unforgeable without performing it), and ephemerality (10-day expiry +
Prune — sealed against casual observation, cheap to store).

## 3. Dependencies

Constructed with VOID; reaches ZHENG for Bao lookups by soul; Reacts both
parties' SHAs.

## 4. State

`Zheng`, `Void`, `_encryptions` (index → {Data, Expiry}), `_indexes`
(capped at MotzkinPrime entries — `TooManyCrypts`).

Effects: `Crypt` (internal) — the alternating loop: Rod.React(hashKey(key),
cone.Channel) seeds; per 8 bytes Cone.React then Rod.React advance the
keystream; indexable = the final _e. `hashKey` = keccak(key) mod
MotzkinPrime. `Decrypt` recomputes the same stream and deletes on expiry
read. `Encapsulate` is the self-to-self variant folding Gamma/Rho/Upsilon/
Ohm into chromosomes. Overloads `Encrypt/Decrypt(string Key, …)` wrap the
bytes forms.
> ng-correction (session-59 fork, harness set-3 steps 11–12, pin 27,674,000):
> (a) the keystream is a DETERMINISTIC modexp orbit — SHA.React(Pi, Theta)
> is pure math over the per-SHA `Channel` constant, so the Encrypt index and
> the Decrypt plaintext preview EXACTLY equal the committed results (no
> shared-entropy divergence), and one string-Encrypt meters +4 units to the
> contract (string → bytes → Crypt + hashKey each run _mintToCap). (b) the
> party gate reads the SHIO owners MAP (the view `owner(address)`) — the
> wallet that created the LAU passes because SHIOFactory.New adds TX.ORIGIN
> to every SHIO; the tx.origin leg of the onlyOwners modifier is irrelevant
> here. Encrypt gates on the FROM side, Decrypt on the TO side (a third EOA
> encrypting as you, or YOU decrypting your own sent note, revert
> NotParty). (c) the index is NOT pair-bound: a TO-owner feeding a foreign
> index through the swapped pair does NOT revert — it answers XOR garbage. 
> (d) an entry past its 10-day expiry decrypts ONCE more (the delete fires
> after the read), then answers empty bytes. (e) **Prune is defective — the
> wrong key domain**: it iterates `_encryptions[i]` by ARRAY POSITION while
> entries are keyed by KEYSTREAM INDEX, so it mangles the private
> enumeration but leaves BOTH live and EXPIRED entries decryptable
> (fork-pinned: "stale one" still decrypted after Prune).
> ng-correction addendum (session-61 fork, agent_runner --first-contact,
> pin 27,674,000): a LIVE (unexpired) entry SURVIVES a committed Decrypt —
> deletion keys on `Expiry < block.timestamp` (encrypt.sol:121), NOT on
> read; do NOT generalize (d) to fresh entries (a live note re-decrypts to
> the exact plaintext for its whole 10-day life — pinned via the runner's
> post-send re-preview). Also pinned: one string-DECRYPT meters +4 units
> (the same quadruple chain as Encrypt).
